# 🔓 Disable Deployment Protection

Your website is currently protected by Vercel Authentication. To make it publicly accessible:

## Steps to Disable:

### 1. Go to Vercel Dashboard
Visit: https://vercel.com/lunexhubs-projects/testing

### 2. Click on "Settings"
In the top navigation bar

### 3. Scroll to "Deployment Protection"
Look for the section called "Deployment Protection"

### 4. Disable Protection
- Click on "Deployment Protection"
- Turn OFF "Vercel Authentication"
- OR Select "No Protection"  
- Click "Save"

### 5. Done!
Your website will now be publicly accessible without login!

---

## Alternative: Keep Protection but Allow Access

If you want to keep some protection:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Deployment Protection**
2. Choose **Password Protection** instead
3. Set a simple password
4. Share the password with users

---

## After Disabling:

Your website will be accessible at:
**https://testing-beta-seven-35.vercel.app**

No login required! ✅
